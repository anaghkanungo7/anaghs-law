## LinkedIn

I named an obvious observation after myself. For science? Mostly curiosity :)

Meet Anagh's Law: “Clear the maximum, clear the rest.”

If a bus clears a tunnel, a vehicle contained within its clearance envelope clears it too - provided the same placement and path are actually allowed. Lower volume or mass alone isn't enough.

The mathematics is elementary containment. I haven't discovered a new theorem. The experiment is whether a precise explanation, a memorable name, and a public record can help an idea travel while keeping its attribution intact.

I've published the definition, examples, limits, and a short explanatory note. Now the interesting part: will anyone find the name useful enough to use independently?
